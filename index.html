Velocity FC — 5 Core Rules

1. Ball side squeezes. The whole team shifts toward the ball.
2. Far side tucks in. Do not stay wide when defending.
3. Nearest presses, next covers. Never send two players charging at the same opponent.
4. One defender always stays secure. If one defender steps forward, the other protects the goal.
5. Striker stays available. Help screen passes but remain our counterattack outlet.
